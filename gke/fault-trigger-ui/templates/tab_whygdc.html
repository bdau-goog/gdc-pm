Why Google Distributed Cloud?

You've decided edge AI is the answer for upstream operations. The scenarios above showed what it can do. Here's why GDC specifically — form factor fit, fleet governance, and AI platform depth.

01 — FORM FACTOR FIT
Validated hardware for your topology

GDC runs on validated hardware across three deployment shapes — connected rack, software on your own hardware, and fully air-gapped appliance. Match the footprint to your field topology without changing the application stack above it.

🔗 Connected — RTOC with network
💾 Software-only — your own hardware
🔒 Air-gapped — isolated facilities
02 — FLEET GOVERNANCE
One control plane. N sovereign edge nodes.

Anthos Config Management and Fleet Management give you one declarative policy for security, configuration, and version consistency across every GDC site — the platform and the applications running on it. Company-wide consistency from the same control plane you use for your cloud GKE fleet.

⚠ Scope: IT platform + applications — not OT/PLC/SCADA L1–L2. OT integration still required above this layer.
03 — SOVEREIGN AI PLATFORM
Google's full AI stack — on your terms

The same Google stack on-prem: GKE, AlloyDB Omni, Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Train centrally on your fleet data, deploy base models to each edge node with local fine-tuning for site-specific conditions, govern rollouts and rollbacks centrally, propagate findings fleet-wide.
