Panel 1 of 3 — The Opportunity

Maximum Production. Maximum Care.

Oil price is up. Pad Alpha has 6 wells. The instinct is: run everything faster. But these wells are not equal.

Every oil well produces associated gas — gas the reservoir controls, not you. GOR (Gas-Oil Ratio) tells you how much gas per barrel. A well with GOR 1,350 produces 3× more gas per barrel than a well with GOR 450. You cannot change that. But you can choose which wells get the gas budget.

"Not all barrels cost the same gas."
A-3 produces 3× more oil per unit of gas budget than A-5. That asymmetry is the optimization opportunity.
The question is not whether to push harder.
It is: which wells get more gas budget, and which give way?
Panel 2 of 3 — The Tradeoff

Three Ceilings You Cannot Ignore.

Push all 6 wells harder and you hit the midstream gas contract. Push the wrong ones too hard and you burn out the motors.
