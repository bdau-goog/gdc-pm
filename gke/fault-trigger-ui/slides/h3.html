THE SCENARIO

Maximum Production. Maximum Care.

Oil price is up. Pad Alpha has 6 wells. The instinct is to run everything faster.
But these wells are not equal — and the gas budget forces a choice.

Every oil well produces associated gas — gas the reservoir controls, not you. GOR (Gas-Oil Ratio) tells you how much gas per barrel. A well with GOR 1,350 produces 3× more gas per barrel than a well with GOR 450. You cannot change that. But you can choose which wells get the gas budget.
