When Should You Consider Using GDC?

Compliance & Sovereignty

Regulatory requirements, contractual obligations, and national data residency laws require operational data — well histories, production records, unstructured field documents — never leave the operator's physical infrastructure.

Survivability

Maintain operations even during network outages — full workload continuity, disconnected-mode AI.

Latency

Process data and make decisions in near real time — inference at the source, sub-100ms response.

Data Gravity

Acoustic logs, seismic surveys, completion records, shift notes, and realtime historian streams are generated at the field. Processing them at the edge eliminates cloud egress cost and network dependency — and keeps sensitive operational data sovereign.
