THE HOOK

One Signature, Two Causes

Both failure modes produce the identical Pump Inlet Pressure + Amps decline on an intake-only sensor string. The cause — and the correct action — are not in these numbers.

NOMINAL FAULT ONSET
GAS INTERFERENCE · HIGH GVF
Free gas at pump intake — hydraulic unloading
✔ VFD trim 52→44 Hz — lowers intake draw, gas vents up the annulus. Well stays online. Zero-cost SCADA command.
RESERVOIR DRAWDOWN · SAND RISK
Critically low dynamic fluid level — sand mobilization risk
✘ VFD trim — drops tubing velocity below transport limit; sand bridges above pump, blocking restart. ✔ Reduce speed in steps, holding above minimum cooling-flow Hz · verify fluid level from last sonic survey before any further reduction.

THE MOAT

STATE vs. CONTEXT
